A NECESSARY DISTINCTION

Keeping data in place is a boundary, not a guarantee.

Source records staying local can reduce unnecessary movement. It does not, by itself, protect against information being exposed through model updates or a trained model.

Every deployment still needs its own safeguards, risk assessment, and review of what can leave each environment and what can be released.

Privacy is a system property. Model and update risks belong in the design from the start.
